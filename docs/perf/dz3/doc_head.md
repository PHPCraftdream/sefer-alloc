# DZ-3 — the foreign-free route pin cache becomes direct-mapped (`perf(runtime)`)

**Class:** `perf(runtime)` — the per-thread route pin cache of the foreign-free path
(`src/registry/heap_core_xthread/route_pin_cache.rs`) changes from 4 slots with round-robin eviction to 32
direct-mapped slots; the hit path stays instruction-identical, the miss path is not touched. Tables and the summary CSV
are generated by `scripts/dz3_report.mjs` from `docs/perf/dz3/`; the prose cites them. Platform: Linux (WSL2) only.

## 0. Summary

1. **The loss (PC-1 / PC-2).** A producer that frees blocks of one owner out of 5 or more segments in a non-sequential
   order missed the 4-slot cache on every free (cyclic order: hit rate 0.00 at m = 5, 8, 16 segments; random order
   0.79 / 0.50 / 0.25). A miss costs a directory lookup under a shard lock, a reference-count RMW and two sweeps:
   about 440-460 extra Ir per free (extra Ir per free over the sequential order of the same run: {{EX5}} at m = 5 cyclic).
   Against mimalloc the foreign free of the base was {{SLOW_RANGE}} times slower in those orders (m = 5 ... 64), while
   the sequential order was {{SEQ_FAST}} times faster than mimalloc.
2. **The change.** 32 direct-mapped slots indexed by `(base / SEGMENT) & 31`: consecutive 4 MiB segments of one owner
   fall into distinct slots, a lookup is one index and one compare (no scan), a miss evicts the slot's occupant. The
   sweep of dead (unlinked) pins is bounded to 4 occupied slots per call from a persistent cursor (it examines every
   occupied slot of a cache that holds at most 4 pins). The ghost events of the pin ledger stay at the same sites.
3. **Result (Linux, section A).** For m = 4 ... 16 segments every order reaches a hit rate of at least 0.99, i.e. only the first touch of each segment misses (counting build, asserted by the script).
   Time per foreign free, base -> change (mimalloc): m = 5 cyclic {{CYC5}}; m = 16 cyclic {{CYC16}}; m = 16 random
   {{RND16}}; m = 8 random {{RND8}}. The sequential order moves by {{SEQ_RANGE}} of base in time
   (m = 16: {{SEQ16}}) and by {{SEQ_IR}} in program instructions.
4. **What is not fixed.** At m = 64 the 32 slots thrash like the 4 did: cyclic {{CYC64}}, random {{RND64}} (hit rate
   random {{HIT64R}}); the change is within the run-to-run spread there (cyclic, change / base: {{C64C}} in section C and {{C64A}} in
   section A, the same cell in two runs) and far above mimalloc. A cache that large would raise the retention bound further (section 3) or needs a
   different structure.
5. **Controls.** larson / mstress at T = 2 and T = 4 (section D): no difference readable (ranges overlap in every cell).
   The program instructions of the sequential order differ by under 0.3 % (section 0 item 3); the deterministic CI judge (`npm run iai`) runs on CI.
6. **Cost.** The retention bound of dead descriptors held by an idle thread grows with the slot count: 4 slots held up
   to about 1.16 MiB, 32 slots hold up to about 9.3 MiB per thread in the worst case (every class in use; typically far
   less; section 3). It is one constant (`PIN_CACHE_SLOTS`); 16 slots lose the m = 16 cyclic case (section B).

## 1. What was measured

- **Identity.** Base: `main` @ `6b8dd8e3` (the pin cache is identical to `c0ee7dfb`). Change: git tree
  `f81db41b431d18898b71e885e68852c76a1e5e40` (the working tree of branch `g1-dz3` at measurement time), recorded by
  `git write-tree` before the run; binary hashes, rustc and tool versions: `final_ident.txt`. The measured probe is
  `examples/g1_rhythm_probe.rs pincycle <m> <order> [mi]` (the `mi` arm was added for this report; the measured code
  path of the sefer arm is unchanged). Time builds use `production`; counting builds use
  `production bench-internals internals alloc-stats` (pin cache hits / misses). rustc 1.98.1, Linux 6.18 (WSL2),
  i7-11800H, shared with other sessions.
- **Entry point.** `SeferAlloc` as the `#[global_allocator]` of the probe process (the probe frees through
  `GlobalAlloc::dealloc` on another thread, the real foreign-free path `HeapCore::publish_foreign`); mimalloc through the
  same probe with `mi`.
- **Shape.** The owner allocates `(m + 2) x 4096` blocks of 1 KiB, `m` segments of 2048 freed blocks each are chosen; one
  producer thread frees them in order `seq` (segment by segment), `cyclic` (one block per segment in turn) or `random`
  (fixed seed). Time per free = producer wall time / (m x 2048).
- **Statistics.** Time: medians of 9 interleaved processes per arm and point. Hit rate: counting build, every point
  twice, asserted equal. Extra Ir per free: callgrind program totals, `(total(order) - total(seq)) / (m x 2048)` of the
  same arm (the set-up is identical across orders). MT controls: medians of 7 interleaved runs.
