## 3. Reading

**The mechanism ran as labelled (path-activation oracle, R30-8).** In every benefit cell B visits 0 pending words
and reads the summary instead: `multiseg` 5 116 summary words against A's 319 063 pending words; `seg_cycle`
16 088 against 1 004 503; `growth16_n` 11 127 against 524 800. `routed_scans` is equal in both arms (99 / 275 /
2 048). The producers show exactly the registered work:
- same-word: 4 096 summary loads and 64 RMWs, one per newly nonempty pending word;
- distinct-word: 4 096 loads and 4 096 RMWs.

The quiescent gauge `quiescent_summary_missing_bits` is 0 on B. These are hard assertions in the judge and in the
table script, not prose.

**The producer price is the scheme's own cost, not noise.**
- Same-word: +8.125 Ir per free. That is consistent with one summary-word load and test on every remote free,
  plus one cold `fetch_or(Release)` per new word, amortised over 64 frees.
- Distinct-word: +16 Ir per free, where every free pays both the load and the RMW.

Both fall on the hot remote-free path, and both exceed budgets that were frozen before any candidate data. The
same-word budget has been +6 since R19, and the 2026-10-12 arbiter rejected a post-hoc relative budget. Repeated
arms agree exactly: B1 = B2 and A1 = A2 in every cell, with one exception, `mimalloc_bootstrap_proxy` B2 (last
paragraph).

**The +99 in strict trim and bounded maintenance is producer work too.** Both cells free 20 blocks remotely before
the trim. On B the oracle records 20 summary loads and 1 RMW there, and owner discovery runs through 1 summary word
instead of 1 pending word. So the delta is the hinted producer path plus the summary discovery step, not the trim
scan reading the prefix. The run does not decompose the 99 further; `profiles/` in the artifact is the starting
point for that.

**Construction is +42 Ir, fixed per instance.** The same +42 appears at n, 2n and 4n in all four hot families, so the
per-operation slope stays neutral (Δ 0) while every instance pays 42 Ir more. The budget for net construction is
0 + T_s (10). This is the C‴ counterpart of C''’s +641 and C′’s +569. It is smaller, but it is still not zero.

**`mimalloc_bootstrap_proxy` is nondeterministic in this harness.** B2 measured 13 530 Ir. A1, A2, B1 and C0 all
measured 13 050. B1 and B2 ran the same binary (sha256 `0e4993e2…8262`). The cell is one 4 MiB `MiMalloc`
alloc/free (`benches/perf_gate_iai.rs`) and does not call SeferAlloc. Under the frozen exact-zero rule this fails mode 1, but it is not a C‴ effect: the B1 pair of the
same cell is 0, and the verdict is NO-GO on the mode 2 price checks regardless. The cause is not established, and
the per-cell profiles of the mimalloc cells are not retained. This is filed as `docs/perf/OPEN_ITEMS.md` item 89,
because any later gate that reuses the exact-zero mimalloc rule inherits it.

**Non-claims.** These are deterministic Ir under Callgrind only: nothing about native latency, cross-core
contention, or coherence traffic of the summary words (native axis deferred). The benefit result stands as
measured, an order-of-magnitude Ir reduction in owner discovery. What it rejects is C‴ as specified, as a
default-path change, at the frozen price.

## 4. Decision

The owner decision of 2026-10-14 ("удаляем и флаг и функционал") is:
- C‴ is not merged and PR #29 is closed;
- the `RemoteFreeDiscovery` flag fallback (P1-7) is not built;
- the default discovery path stays the fixed pending-word scan of `main`.

Three candidates have now been gated and rejected on the producer price: R19 C′ +9.03, R20 C'' +13.03, R20 C‴
+8.125 / +16 Ir per remote free. Every producer-marked summary puts a summary-word access on every remote free. A
future proposal has to move that cost off the producer, not tune it. The one idea on record that changes only
the owner side is R17-OPT-01 (an owner load before the empty-word swap). It was never gated on its own and goes
back to the unowned list of `docs/perf/OPEN_ITEMS.md` item 88.

- the C3 harness (prereg, judge, runner, verifier, cells, oracle example);
- the P1-2 actual-source Loom model and the P1-3 strict trim from payload start, both of which concern the shipped

`docs/CROSS_THREAD_STATE_MACHINES.md` §10 is marked REJECTED, not in effect. No C‴ hook exists on `main`: the
