- Entry point: real `SeferAlloc` through the `#[inline(never)]` shims of `examples/g1_big_judge.rs` (the layer the change ships at). 12 blocks x 2 repeats per arm and cell, cells shuffled per block, the four arms (base, x, base2, mimalloc from the base binary) in a rotation by block.
- Cold time = the first repetition, all phases (allocate + touch + free). Per block and arm the minimum over the repeats; d = ln(x / base); cell = exp(mean d) with a 95 % t-interval (11 degrees of freedom, 2.201). A/A = base2 / base.
  Faults, RSS and `anon_huge_peak` (THP-backed bytes sampled right after a touch phase) are medians over the runs of a cell.

## 4. Reading of the failures

Every failed gate is a T2 RSS limit; the largest overshoot is 142 KiB of 5 limits, against RSS medians of up to 4101 MiB. The limits are exact multiples of 2 MiB (one huge page per hinted reservation, which also holds the header page), and a median RSS carries a few tens of KiB of unrelated allocations, so the preregistered zero-tolerance reading fails by margins at the measurement granularity. T0, T1, the control and every other gate pass. By the letter the verdict stays NO-GO; the substance is a very large benefit at a cost equal to the mechanism's bound, and the decision to accept that goes to the owner.

## 5. Decision

**NO-GO.** No change to `src/` is merged; the patch stays on branch `g1-j13` only as the measured candidate.
Not claimed: Windows (no-op by construction), THP modes `always`/`never`, workloads touching a small part of many big blocks beyond the `edge` cell.
