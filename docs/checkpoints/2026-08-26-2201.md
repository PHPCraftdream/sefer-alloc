   from wave 1's (numerics/docs/tests) — regression-hunting wave 1's own fixes,

   breaking-change `!` commit prefix even though unpublished — same before-first-release
   discipline as the earlier `Copy` removal.

   leaving them as historical record in the live TaskList — commit messages/git log remain
   the durable record instead.

   (including the `address(k) = base + k*block_size` derivation) in exactly one place —
   `class_for`'s own `# Preconditions` section — and reduced every other site (crate doc,

   CHANGELOG) to a one-line pointer, rather than trying to shorten all six sites independently.
