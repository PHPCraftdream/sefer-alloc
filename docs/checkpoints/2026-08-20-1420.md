## Decisions

- **#1190 answered NO, and the record names BOTH flag values as illegal for a non-2-MiB HugeTLB
  mapping.** The `granted_huge: false` side was the real hole — `false` does not remove the
  kernel's munmap alignment requirement, it removes the assert that would have caught the
  mis-shape. Wording is "can leak", not "will leak", with the reason stated inline: no CI host
  configures a non-2-MiB pool and the crate never creates one (`MAP_HUGE_2MB` hardcoded).
- **#1234 resolved as (b): no `#1174` reference added to `decommit_outcome.rs`.** Adding one
  could not make an immutable commit body true, and the fact already lives in FIVE files —
  the #1161 drift class with six prior reproductions.
- **Counts in prose are being replaced by commands, not by new numbers.** "42 citations" →
  a census command (#1227); "70 `[T]` cards" → two commands that must agree (#1233). Rationale
  each time: a number typed into prose is a second copy of a fact, and both of these went stale
  the day they were written.
- **Line-number citations are being replaced by symbol names, not by corrected numbers**
  (#1229/F8). A replacement number would be the fourth generation of that defect; the original
  citation drifted four minutes after it was written.
- **Items 90/91 deliberately NOT closed** although their stated conditions are met: closing
  under R34-24 means moving narratives to `RESOLVED.md` and editing the lookup table, and cards
  93/94 assign that to a coordinated refresh. The agent proposed this and I agreed.
